Load your inventory spreadsheet

The app keeps everything in your inventory spreadsheet. Choose your copy of RRR Coffee Inventory Master.xlsx (or any .xlsx with the same columns) to get started.

The first sheet needs the header row Origin, Varietal, Provider, Arrival Date, Cultivar, Tasting Notes, Cupping Score, Roast Level, Label Color by Roast Level, Lbs, Cost per lb, Diff to Ave, MSRP, COGS, Margin, SKU, UPC in columns A to Q. Already have the file somewhere on this computer? Point to it in Settings instead.
