JUST SHOOT ME RECEIPT MAKER
===========================

Start it
  Online: open https://emmersontools.com/jsm-receipt-maker/

  On your Mac (no internet needed): double-click
  "Start Receipt Maker.command" in this folder (apps/jsm-receipt-maker).
  A Terminal window opens and the app opens in your browser
  (http://127.0.0.1:8765). Keep the Terminal window open while you work;
  close it when you're done.

  If macOS says the file can't be opened, right-click it, choose Open,
  then Open again. You only need to do that once.

  Or, from Terminal in this folder:
    python3 server.py

  The app's page itself lives in public/jsm-receipt-maker/ with the rest
  of the website.

Make a receipt
  1. Fill in the customer. Receipt # (JSM26-0001, JSM26-0002, ...) and
     today's date are filled in for you.
  2. Add items:
     - Firearm / Accessory: you set the description, serial #, qty and price.
       Firearms get a $50 transfer fee per firearm automatically (untick the
       box to leave it off).
     - Service: classes, gunsmithing, etc. You set the description and price.
     - Transfer Fee: $50 per firearm, for guns bought elsewhere.
     - Shipping: $25 per item shipped, or type an override total.
  3. Click "Download PDF". The browser downloads the PDF (usually to
     Downloads) and the receipt is added to the Receipt log.

Purchase receipts (buying from a customer)
  Click "Purchase Receipt" at the top. Enter the seller (name, address,
  ID) and each firearm or accessory you're buying with the price you're
  paying. There are no transfer or shipping fees. The PDF has a seller
  certification and signature lines, and is numbered JSMP26-0001,
  JSMP26-0002, ... separately from sales.

PDFs are named like "Reed Harmon Receipt 10-27-2026 JSM26-0001.pdf"
or "Sam Seller Purchase Receipt 10-28-2026 JSMP26-0001.pdf".

Receipt log
  The panel on the right lists every receipt you've downloaded, newest
  first, with its number, customer, date and total. Search by name or
  number, or show only sales or purchases.
  - Open: puts that receipt back on screen so you can download it again
    or fix it (downloading again with the same number updates the log).
  - Remove: takes it off the log (doesn't touch any PDF).
  - Export CSV: the whole log as a spreadsheet.
  - Back up log: saves the log to a file. Do this now and then.
  - Restore / import: load a backup, or select old receipt PDFs to add
    them to the log by file name (so numbering continues from them).
  The log is stored in the browser you use, so the copy on your Mac and
  the online copy each keep their own log. Use Back up / Restore to move
  it between them.

To change the fee amounts or business details, edit the lines near the
top of the <script> section in public/jsm-receipt-maker/index.html (TRANSFER_FEE, SHIPPING_FEE,
BUSINESS).
