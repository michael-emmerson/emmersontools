Maturity levels

Scoring. Capability score is the selected level (1–5). Pillar score is the average of its three capabilities. Overall score is the average of the seven pillars, each weighted equally. Overall level: below 1.5 = Initial, 1.5–2.4 = Experimenting, 2.5–3.4 = Operational, 3.5–4.4 = Scaled, 4.5+ = Transformational.

Governance gate. An organization cannot be rated Operational or higher overall while Governance or Data averages below 2.5. Pilots reach production only when an inventory, risk tiering and data ownership exist.

Source. Independently written rubric. The seven-pillar structure and five-level progression follow the widely published shape of common enterprise AI maturity models (Gartner, MITRE, Microsoft); no proprietary content is reproduced. Governance descriptors reference NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001. Scores and notes are saved only in this browser.
